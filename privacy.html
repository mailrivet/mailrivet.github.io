mailrivet privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy describes how the mailrivet project's software handles your data.

What mailrivet accesses

With your consent, mailrivet uses the Gmail API or Microsoft Graph to read messages and threads in your mailbox and, if you grant draft permission, to create draft messages. It does not request permission to send, delete or move email.

Where your data goes

mailrivet runs only on your own computer. Email content is fetched directly from Google or Microsoft to your computer and is not sent to the mailrivet project or any third party by mailrivet. The project operates no servers and collects no telemetry, analytics or usage data.

What is stored, and where

  • OAuth tokens are stored in your operating system's keychain.
  • An account registry (account address, granted scopes, policy mode) and a local audit log of draft activity are stored in files in your home directory. The audit log records identifiers and a keyed hash of each draft, never its content or recipients.

You can delete this data at any time by removing those files and keychain entries, and revoke mailrivet's access from your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Google user data

mailrivet's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the features you invoke on your own computer, and is not transferred, sold, or used for advertising.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/mailrivet/mailrivet.github.io.
